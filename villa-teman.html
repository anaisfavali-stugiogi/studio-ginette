Villa Teman
2024 · Interior Design
Location
Bali, Indonesia
Year
2024
Scope
Full Interior Design
Category
Residential

Villa Teman is a contemporary holiday home imagined as a calm escape from the pace of everyday life . The intention was to create a space that feels modern and refined while preserving the warmth and ease that make a house instantly feel like home.

Natural stone, sleek wood, soft linen and a sophisticated color palette with a touch of deep black bring depth and softness to the clean architectural lines. Large openings invite light throughout the day and create a seamless connection between the interiors and the surrounding landscape.

The result is a home that feels calm, sun-filled, and deeply inviting balancing modern simplicity with a sense of comfort, intimacy and slow living.
