About

Anaïs Favali
the eye behind the studio

Studio Ginette was born from a love of the everyday interior, the home you return to, the space you wake up in. Founded in Bali, the studio specialises in residential interiors that feel genuinely lived-in and deeply personal. Inspired by the tropical way of living, each space is shaped by a blend of natural warmth and French refinement.

We work on homes, apartments, and short-let properties — transforming them through considered design, thoughtful material choices, and an instinct for what makes a space feel right. Whether it's a full redesign or a targeted renovation upgrade, every project starts with listening.

What We Do

Our services

01
Home & Apartment Design
Full-service interior design for primary residences and apartments. From mood board, 3D renders to final styling, we handle every detail so your home becomes exactly what you imagined.
02
Airbnb-Ready Interiors
We design and fully-furnish short-let properties to feel effortlessly welcoming and photographically beautiful, maximising both guest experience and rental appeal.
03
Renovation Upgrades
Targeted interventions that transform a space without a full overhaul. New materials, finishes, lighting, and furniture we handle the changes that make the biggest difference.
04
Styling & Finishing
The final step, but not the least! Through object selection, art placement, textiles, and the carefully chosen details we turn a designed room into a real home.
05
Concept & Mood Direction
For clients at the start of their journey. We build a complete visual direction: palette, materials, furniture style, so you know exactly where you're headed before committing.
06
Design Consultancy
A focused session to get an expert eye on your space. Ideal for homeowners who want honest guidance on what to change, keep, and invest in. We do offer this service also remotly.
